# Sample credits

Every recording here is public domain (CC0 or PD). They were trimmed, looped, loudness-matched and
re-encoded for Code Room; `manifest.json` records the source file and the measured tuning of each.

- **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner, CC0 1.0 —
  https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
  Steinway B grand piano, concert harp, kalimba (Kenya), glockenspiel, hand chimes, singing wine
  glasses, bowed vibraphone, bowed psaltery, Nepalese hand bells, bell tree, gongs, timpani roll,
  suspended cymbal, mark trees (wind chimes), ocean drum.
- **Wikimedia Commons** (CC0 / public domain):
  - "Ocean Waves on a Tropical Beach.ogg" (CC0) → `ocean.m4a`
  - "Water flowing pouring trickling.ogg" (public domain) → `water.m4a`
  - "Rain against the window.ogg" (public domain) → `rain.m4a`
  - "Howling wind.ogg" (CC0) → `wind.m4a`

Note on pitch: VCSL names several instruments by their written pitch; the manifest's `root` is the
measured sounding pitch (hand chimes, glockenspiel, glasses, bowed vibes, kalimba and psaltery
sound an octave above their names).
