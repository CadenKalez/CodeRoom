# Sample credits

Every recording here is public domain (CC0 or PD). They were trimmed, looped, loudness-matched and
re-encoded for Code Room; `manifest.json` records the source file and the measured tuning of each.

- **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner, CC0 1.0 —
  https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
  Steinway B grand piano, concert harp, kalimba (Kenya), glockenspiel, hand chimes, singing wine
  glasses, bowed vibraphone, bowed psaltery, Nepalese hand bells, bell tree, gongs, timpani roll,
  suspended cymbal, mark trees (wind chimes), ocean drum.
- **Wikimedia Commons** (CC0 / public domain):
  - "Ocean Waves on a Tropical Beach.ogg" (CC0) → `ocean.m4a`
  - "Water flowing pouring trickling.ogg" (public domain) → `water.m4a`
  - "Rain against the window.ogg" (public domain) → `rain.m4a`
  - "Howling wind.ogg" (CC0) → `wind.m4a`

- **VCSL** (as above): more of the Steinway B, every whole tone from A#1 to D6 (soft velocity), so the
  felt piano never stretches a note more than a semitone.
- **University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples** — "may be downloaded and
  used for any projects, without restrictions" — https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
  Violin (arco, mf, with vibrato) and flute (vibrato, mf): the chromatic runs were split into single
  notes, every other semitone kept, each measured for pitch (the manifest's `root`), faded and
  loudness-matched → `violin_*.m4a`, `flute_*.m4a`.
- **Thunder** (Wikimedia Commons), cut into single strikes with slow fades in and out (no hard edges),
  low-passed to 4 kHz, gently limited and matched in low-band loudness → `thunder_*.m4a`:
  - "Storm thunderbolts.ogg" by stephan (public domain) → `thunder_7`, `thunder_8`, `thunder_9`
  - "Thunderstorm after hot summer day 17 minutes 01 of 04.ogg" / "… 04 of 04.ogg" by stephan (public domain) → `thunder_10` … `thunder_13`
  - "Light Rain Distant Thunder July 5th 2016.wav" by kvgarlic (CC0) → `thunder_1`, `thunder_2`, `thunder_3`
  - "Booming Thunder 2023-10-09 2-07-34 am.ogg" by KTerPalmers (CC BY-SA 4.0) → `thunder_0`
  - "Lightning Recorded In Groningen 2018-05-13 01.wav" / "… 02.wav" by A. Whyborn (CC BY-SA 4.0) → `thunder_4`, `thunder_5`
  - "Nosferatu thunderclap - Richard Humphries.wav" by Richard Humphries (CC BY 4.0) → `thunder_6`
  (CC BY-SA: the processed thunder files are shared under the same license.)

Note on pitch: VCSL names several instruments by their written pitch; the manifest's `root` is the
measured sounding pitch (hand chimes, glockenspiel, glasses, bowed vibes, kalimba and psaltery
sound an octave above their names).
